April 29th, 2000 First Blue Marlin on the Seahawk....287 pounds...not a monster, but good for openers .My poor gaff job probably caused him not to exceed 300 pounds. We started out trolling from the harbor to VV buoy , then headed inshore to Red Hill , where we switched to Wahoo gear, and worked the 50-100 fathom curve all the way down to the Limp Dick Man lava flow, about 16 miles with no hits . We then trolled out to UU buoy , about three miles, and upon no signs for Aku. Captain Brian called to say that he was marking bait nearby ...in short order we found the school . Mike caught one Aku and two small Ahis...we rigged up three found Aku and ten pound Ahi for trollers and headed offshore from UU .Mike caught another Ahi for food. Mike called his rigged bait Ahi ,Rover . Mike was sitting forward on the console cooler working on his vocabulary lesson and asked me a question . I turned over the wheel to John and was sitting next to Mike . John noticed #64 rubber band holding the Port tag line was stretched out to two feet ! he hauled down the lanyard , popped the band, fed the bait back , then free spooled the 130 ...I got back on the wheel and John looked at me ,he said GO . He did and I did , and we hooked up ! About a thirty minute fight and he came in green . John handled the leader and I had a good Fly gaff shot about him low and a bit aft of his right pectoral. With that all hell breaks loose and with the one inch gaff line tight as a fiddle string ...quite a sight ! Lots of water being thrown . I load up the 410 snack charmer and settle his hash as I board side. Until then he was towing the Seahawk around at a speed of two knots or so . Then we had the job of getting him into the boat. It took four attempts before we finally got him in . Three attempts we got the marlin with the best gaff runners for instruction. The only way we made the Marlin in the boat was getting Mike in the act . His 150 pounds made the difference . We pulled up the fighting chair to make room for our guest in the cockpit . We then trolled back to Kona without another strike , but wow , what a day. Tim and Linda were married today , probably about the time we boated the Blue . Mike's note ... My opinion is that was some ass whooping fish ! I was up in the Bow when it happened with Rover . The Marlin was so close to the Tuna I was holding , I almost fainted .
